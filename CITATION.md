# Citation guidance

Until publication-author metadata are confirmed, cite the research repository by its maintainer handle, title, year, URL and exact commit SHA. This is a repository citation, not a claim of sole human authorship or scientific priority.

> judogaaa (maintainer), *The covering number C(14,5,3)*, research draft and verification software, 2026. https://github.com/judogaaa/covering-c14-5-3

Cite the historical construction and source literature separately. No DOI or refereed publication has been established.
