# Reviewing the proof

The most valuable contributions are independently reproducible checks, counterexamples, and corrections to the mathematical argument or exact enumeration.

When reporting a problem, please include:

- the proof section, file, or algorithm involved;
- the smallest counterexample or the exact logical gap;
- the commit ID and commands, Python/compiler versions, and exit status if relevant;
- a minimal relevant log excerpt and the expected behavior.

A timeout or failed run should be reported as an incomplete verification, not as a mathematical counterexample. Avoid modifying certificate data to match expected counts without explaining why the new data are complete and valid. Changes to the mathematical logic or enumeration should be accompanied by a fresh full audit and a corresponding update to the proof text.
